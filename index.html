About

Memo: Random is the creation of founder and host, Trevor H. Rudolph, whose career spans the White House, a cybersecurity startup, and a Fortune 500 multinational. Trevor's had a hand in crafting government policy, building a startup from the ground up, and helping a multinational navigate geopolitical tensions between two great powers. Trevor's goal is to bring you a fresh perspective on geopolitics in a unique and interactive environment with Memo: Random.

Its sibling show, Global Coup, runs the same subject matter through a competitive format instead.

Today's geopolitics shows can be a bit self-important and stuffy — and the subject matter is genuinely complex, sometimes even scary, while the average person barely gets a voice in it. Memo: Random is my attempt at a fresh approach: earnestly analyze the key issues of our time, without taking myself or the conversation too seriously.— Trevor H. Rudolph, Host
