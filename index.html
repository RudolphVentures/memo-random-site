About

Memo: Random is the creation of founder and host Trevor H. Rudolph, whose career spans the White House, technology, and the Fortune 500 business world. As a former government official and global government affairs executive, Trevor has crafted government policy, helped build a startup, and counseled multinational businesses on how to navigate geopolitical tensions between great powers. Trevor serves as a Senior Non-Resident Fellow at the Atlantic Council GeoTech Center. His goal is to bring you a fresh perspective on geopolitics in a unique and interactive environment with Memo: Random.

Your Host

Trevor H. Rudolph is a global technology and cybersecurity policy executive whose career spans North America, Europe, and Asia. He currently serves as a Senior Non-Resident Fellow at the Atlantic Council GeoTech Center, and previously served as Schneider Electric's first Vice President for Global Digital Policy & Regulation, Chief Operating Officer of cybersecurity startup WhiteHawk, and Chief of the White House's Cyber and National Security Unit at OMB, where he architected the President's Cybersecurity National Action Plan. A two-time Federal 100 Award winner and CISSP, he holds degrees from the College of William & Mary and Georgetown University.
